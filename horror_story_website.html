The Voice Between the Trees

At 1:17 a.m., Arjun left the campsite to find the road. His phone showed no signal, but the flashlight still worked. Twenty minutes into the walk, he heard someone whisper, “Arjun.”

He assumed it was his friend playing a prank. Then the voice came again—from behind him.

“The voice sounded exactly like mine.”

He ran toward the lights of the campsite. Every few minutes, the voice followed, always a few metres behind. When he finally reached the tent, everyone was asleep.

In the morning, they found a second set of footprints around the tent. They began at the forest edge and stopped directly beside Arjun's sleeping bag.

Case 027

Room 307 Wasn't There

The old hospital had been closed for eight years. During a routine inspection, a guard noticed a strange detail on a photocopied floor plan: Room 307.

The third floor had Rooms 301 through 306, followed by a sealed stairwell. There was no 307.

At 3:03 a.m., the guard's camera captured him standing in front of a newly visible door. The footage contains 19 seconds of static. When it returns, he is standing at the opposite end of the corridor.

On the wall behind him: “307 — PLEASE KNOCK.”

The building was demolished two weeks later. According to the archive, the door was never found again.

Case 041

The Guest Who Checked Out Twice

Every hotel has an old register. This one had a name that appeared twice: R. Sen — Room 12.

The first entry was dated 1994. The second was dated 2024. Same room. Same signature. Same ink style.

The night clerk said a man matching the description had stayed for exactly one night. At checkout, he asked a single question:

“Is the corridor still there after midnight?”

Staff laughed. The next morning, Room 12 was empty. The bed was made. But the guest's suitcase remained inside—and every clock in the room had stopped at 12:07.
